# Modern Hungarian Bible translations: links and display

Checked 2026-10-14. Research only, following04-06. No publisher contacted, account
created, text imported or new integration released.

## Conclusion for Élő Víz

Ordinary links to freely accessible, authorized readers are the practical option.
Naming the publisher or adding a source link does not itself authorize copying
Bible text into our reader. Fetching text remotely through an API still needs
appropriate display rights; avoiding a local database does not settle that issue.

Recommend retaining Károli1908 in the study inspector and offering labeled
external modern-translation links. This recommendation is not an implemented
feature. Onsite modern-text display needs an applicable licence/permission or a
specific valid quotation exception; a general arbitrary-passage reader should
not be assumed to qualify as quotation.

## Edition evidence

- **RÚF2014:** [Szentírás's edition register](https://szentiras.eu/forditasok)
  records temporary permission from Magyar Bibliatársulat, not a grant to all
  websites. [Official John3 reader](https://abibliamindenkie.hu/uj/JHN/3)
  is publicly accessible. No general onsite-display permission found in this audit.
- **EFO/ERV-HU:** [licensed distributor record](https://www.biblegateway.com/versions/Hungarian-Bible-Easy-to-Read-Version-ERV-HU/)
  identifies Bible League/WBTC. [Publisher's currently served licence](https://www.bibleleague.org/license-agreement/)
  prohibits server hosting without written permission and directs website-use
  enquiries to the publisher. It permits limited quotations up to1000verses,
  excluding a complete book and exceeding50% of the quoting work, with the
  prescribed notice. Do not interpret this as permission for an unrestricted
  reader. The published policy itself is dated July2015; clarification is needed
  before website text integration. Bible Gateway is an external reading option.
- **Káldi-Neovulgáta, Szent István, Békés-Dalos and Simon Tamás László NT:**
  [Szentírás register](https://szentiras.eu/forditasok) records publisher-specific
  permissions, including2025renewals. It is a licensed external reading option;
  its permissions do not establish our own reuse rights.
- **Biblica Nyitott Újszövetség:** separate modern NT-only edition with open
  CC BY-SA4.0 notice, already researched in04-06; not EFO. Its exact licence and
  source audit offer another path for onsite text after product selection.

## Linking, quotation and integration boundaries

- [CJEU Svensson, C-466/12](https://curia.europa.eu/site/upload/docs/application/pdf/2014-02/cp140020en.pdf):
  ordinary hyperlinks to authorized freely accessible works generally require no
  additional copyright authorization. Use lawful owner/licensed-provider pages;
  do not bypass subscriber/access restrictions. This is the basis for the external
  link recommendation, not a claim of a publisher's express linking licence.
- [Hungarian Copyright Act, sections33–34](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1999-76-00-00):
  faithful excerpts may be quoted with source/author attribution to an extent
  justified by the receiving work's purpose and nature. Attribution is one
  condition, not a blanket full-reader exception.
- [Szentírás API terms](https://szentiras.eu/api): attribution requested, Hungarian
  copyright law remains applicable to downloaded material. API availability is
  not a new open licence for every translation.
- [CJEU VG Bild-Kunst, C-392/19](https://curia.europa.eu/site/upload/docs/application/pdf/2021-03/cp210036en.pdf):
  framing that circumvents effective anti-framing measures requires authorization.
  An iframe is distinct from fetching/re-rendering text. No blanket iframe ban
  inferred; site terms, technical controls and exact implementation need checking.

No full-text approval inferred from free access, noncommercial intent, attribution
or another provider's licence. This audit does not establish that permission
cannot be obtained or that modern-text integration is categorically prohibited.
