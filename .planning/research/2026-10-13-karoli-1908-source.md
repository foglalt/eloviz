# Károli 1908: source and reuse register

**Checked:** 2026-10-13
**Decision:** User selected the revised 1908 Károli text; verify source/reuse first.

## Reuse basis

- [CrossWire HunKar module](https://www.crosswire.org/sword/modules/ModInfo.jsp?modName=HunKar)
  identifies the 1908 revised text, version 2.0, with Distribution License
  **Public Domain**. Its [copyright record](https://www2.crosswire.org/sword/copyright/ModInfoCopyright.jsp?modName=HunKar)
  gives the same declaration and points to the editable source.
- [Upstream configuration](https://github.com/krisek/HunKar/blob/0e244494dd190e4a6b132c1afddfa307e73792f8/hunkar.conf)
  independently carries `DistributionLicense=Public Domain`, `Versification=Calvin`.
- [Digital Bible Society record](https://arc.dbs.org/data/scripture/HUNRKV/)
  also identifies the revised 1908 Károli text as Public Domain.
- Based on these explicit distributor declarations, the historical verse text
  is accepted for local storage, display, caching and redistribution. No modern
  translation, publisher branding, modern headings, cross-reference notes or
  page design is imported. Attribution is retained for provenance even though
  this Public Domain declaration does not impose a separate attribution licence.
- This is a documented source/reuse decision based on the published declarations,
  not an independent legal opinion about every possible jurisdiction.

## Pinned sources

Primary: [krisek/HunKar](https://github.com/krisek/HunKar), commit
`0e244494dd190e4a6b132c1afddfa307e73792f8`, `hunkaroli_rev.osis.xml`.
Raw SHA-256: `d54b72e4a0f7d63f464835a2ee0a988f386eccd40c0bc1dc56dec41e00b2b7bd`.
The upstream README attributes the text to Szentírás KG and Song of Songs to the
Hungarian Bible Society's Károli site. Editorial apparatus is excluded.

Completeness inspection found 66 books, 1189 chapters and 31168 verses, with
three internal omissions: `John.21.1`, `Acts.12.6`, `Acts.15.18`.

Supplement: [thiagobodruk/bible](https://github.com/thiagobodruk/bible), commit
`093b4727bf632181c9e096f4cf4141cc387371d0`, `json/hu_hunk.json`.
SHA-256: `183c1abff5068116e69dfae2224154af7898df539649f5871c82b170fd9936f6`.
Only the three historical Károli verse texts are used from this copy. Their
wording and position were checked against the independently downloaded 2009
Zefania/Unbound Hungarian text and the publisher's current chapter pages:

- [John 21](https://abibliamindenkie.hu/karoli/JHN/21)
- [Acts 12](https://abibliamindenkie.hu/karoli/ACT/12)
- [Acts 15](https://abibliamindenkie.hu/karoli/ACT/15)

The older [Zefania download](https://sourceforge.net/projects/zefania-sharp/files/Bibles/HUN/Hungarian%20Version/)
was used only as cross-check evidence: its empty rights field and old diacritic
encoding (`õ`/`û`) make it a weaker primary source. Its archive SHA-256 is
`205138c1037c2795ded210f86bde31d8adc009b3f889094b16596d6689466944`.
The three supplemental texts agree after correcting those encoding substitutions.
No generated prose or reconstructed verse text is used.

## Numbering and transformations

- Preserve Calvin numbering, not KJV numbering. Ecclesiastes 2:1 contains the
  text often labelled 1:18 in another versification; it is not a missing verse.
- Psalm superscriptions may be numbered verses. Preserve them as source text.
- Normalize XML text-node whitespace, decode XML entities, omit notes/headings,
  and insert only the explicitly documented three missing verses.
- Complete imported edition: **66 books, 1189 chapters, 31171 verses**.
- Normalized full-text SHA-256:
  `6119e14ad2f654678428fe406e586e1bc2908d5b44dd32a7f0555857f184ac9b`.
- Pin raw and normalized checksums; a changed source/parser requires review.
- Per-source manifest, supplemental provenance and attribution are stored in
  `data/bible/karoli-1908-source.json` and in the translation database record.

## Sources rejected for direct ingestion

The current scrollmapper HunKar JSON and getBible/Midvash derivatives reproduce
the same omissions and can also retain empty padding verses from a different
versification. eBible's `/hun/` is a 2021 Biblica New Testament, not Károli 1908.
Do not substitute any of these datasets silently.
