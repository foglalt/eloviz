# Phase 03 Plan 33: Ordered study series

## Objective

Publish long studies as ordered, independently discoverable chapters in a named
series. The first intended content is Hungarian Stephen Bohr study notes; the
user confirms permission and will upload separate chapter PDFs.

## Context

- @.planning/PROJECT.md
- @.planning/ROADMAP.md
- @.planning/STATE.md
- @.planning/phases/03-evergreen-content-library/03-CONTEXT.md
- Reference: https://sumtv.org/study-notes (checked 2026-10-13).

## Tasks

1. Add non-destructive series/membership schema, single-series chapter membership,
   transactional ordering, and public/admin repositories. Preserve legacy links.
2. Add series administration with ordered parts, attach-existing controls,
   accessible move/remove buttons, optional author/source, and add-next shortcuts.
   Study editor selects a series and position; next-part creation carries topics.
3. Add public series index/detail, chapter contents and previous/next navigation,
   chapter-specific canonicals/metadata, series structured data, and sitemap links.
4. Test ordering, publication boundaries, transactions, editor interactions, and
   responsive public/admin pages; record evidence, commit, and push.

## Verification

- Focused series tests and existing automated suites; GTD lint/build verification.
- Additive migration; database transaction probes (reorder, move, remove, rollback).
- Authenticated desktop/390 px editor and public routes; draft/empty exclusion,
  canonical/sitemap/structured data checks and cleanup of synthetic fixtures.

## Success Criteria

- Each chapter keeps its independent study URL/PDF and unique summary.
- Published series display only published chapters with a current PDF.
- Draft chapters retain editorial positions but never leak through navigation.
- Editors create/reorder series and add the next chapter without re-entering membership.
- Existing PDFs, study URLs, topic/video relationships, and legacy related links survive.
