# Phase 03 Plan 30: Add symmetric related content

## Objective

Let editors connect studies to other studies, keep both study-study and
study-video relations visible from either side, and present related studies and
videos as compact sidebar sections on public detail pages.

## Tasks

1. Add an undirected study relation table with canonical pair constraints and
   an additive migration.
2. Extend study validation, atomic saving, admin loading, and the study editor
   with a self-excluding related-study picker.
3. Query symmetric related studies and existing symmetric study-video rows,
   filtering public results to published content only.
4. Move related study/video links into the public study sidebar and move a
   video's related studies into its sidebar for consistent presentation.
5. Verify relation canonicalization, both editing directions, responsive admin
   and public layouts, database migration, and production deployment.

## Success Criteria

- Selecting A from B makes the relationship visible when editing or viewing
  either A or B.
- Study-video selection remains visible from both the study and video sides.
- A study cannot relate to itself and duplicate reverse pairs cannot exist.
- Public sidebar links never expose draft studies or videos.
